Meaningful coverage
Hand-calculated timelines and every specified metric; unsorted and equal
arrivals; priority independence; single process and idle periods; independent
I/O; CPU/I/O reentry; no preemption on arrival or unblock; zero I/O and ordered
simultaneous events; zero and positive switch cost; free first/same-PID dispatch;
PID retained across idle; reserved switching and arrivals during switching;
circular FIFO wrap/full/empty; continuous versus cumulative wait and strict
threshold; empty, malformed, duplicate, overflowing, NUL-containing and oversized
inputs; maximum 1024 processes and 31 bursts; invalid config; tick-limit failure
and exact-limit completion; intentionally damaged conservation invariants.

Actual CLI negative cases returned nonzero status and clear errors. Path-alias
regressions verify that the workload cannot be overwritten by alternate output
spellings. Failed simulations preserve preexisting output contents. Ordinary
and instrumented CLI logs are in cli_checks_regular.txt and
cli_checks_sanitized.txt. cli_checks.txt is the latest harness-generated copy.

Deterministic stress
The C test harness uses xorshift32 with seed 433, generates 256 processes, loads
the generated CSV, and executes it twice with switch cost 1 and tick limit 100000.
Both runs complete all 256 processes.
  Total ticks: 4124 = 3357 RUN + 0 IDLE + 767 SWITCH.
  Context switches: 767.
The harness compares both complete trace streams byte for byte and checks
per-process results and trace accounting. SHA256 hashes independently match:
  2F237EA1B65BF096394F392C1AF5DD77DFF5EBAB7027F9719FA355C309BF6805
See stress_a.trace.csv, stress_b.trace.csv, stress.json, stress_sha256.txt,
and ../workloads/stress_seed_433.csv.
